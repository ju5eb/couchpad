⚡ Faster connection

1. Turn off mobile data for a moment (otherwise the browser keeps using it and can't reach the TV).

2. The TV is showing a code: scan it with your phone's camera to join the TV's own Wi-Fi.

3. If the phone says the network has no internet, choose "Keep connection" or "Use anyway".

4. Come back here and leave this page open: the controller switches by itself.
